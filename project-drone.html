VR Target-Tracking Quadcopter

A quadcopter system exploring human detection, visual tracking and target interaction with an onboard flight controller and ground-side interface.

System

OrangeCube+, SIYI MK15, computer vision and a VR interaction layer form the core of the system.

What I worked on

Perception and target-tracking integration, system debugging, and connecting visual information with the flight-control workflow.

Focus

The project explores the complete loop from sensing a target to estimating its position and using that information for interaction.
