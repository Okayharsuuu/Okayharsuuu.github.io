Cycle GPS Tracker

A practical tracking concept for locating parked bicycles and keeping useful position information available even when connectivity is unreliable.

System

The concept combines a GPS module, a microcontroller, wireless data transfer and a web-facing dashboard.

What I worked on

Explored the hardware/software architecture, real-time coordinate handling and offline coordinate caching.

Focus

The project turns a simple location sensor into a usable tracking workflow rather than treating GPS as an isolated module.
